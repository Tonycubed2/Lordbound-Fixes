LORDBOUND FIXES - FULL LIST OF CHANGES
============================================================
Patch: Lordbound Fixes.esp + patched scripts + copied textures/models, for Lordbound 0.8.1.

1. PLUGIN FIXES (Lordbound Fixes.esp)
------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Masters of Highreach: Lanelle had no reaction line if Fardris's quest (LBMQ05) was the master quest you finished first, so the talk ended and stage 2 was never set; her generic line now covers that case

 2. Fill script properties the authors declared and use but left empty in the CK (quests, scenes, triggers, NPCs); empty properties make stages, scenes and triggers silently do nothing

 3. Just Deserts: 'I had to kill him' / 'war criminal' replies required stage below 100, but killing Sergus sets stage 100, so options 2 and 3 dead-ended; now allowed up to stage 109

 4. The Lost Children: Lanelle's 'follow player to Garza' package only ran while she was inside Highreach, so she stopped following the moment you left town and the Garza scene trigger (needs both of you) never fired

 5. Wrath: Garom's two optional answers at the end had no links back to the remaining choices, so you were dropped into a '...' loop; they now offer the other questions and 'You can count on me'

 6. Kinslayer: after you spare Azgoth and pass Borgog's speech check, his reward line required stage 70 (the 'killed him' stage), so the quest never finished; the reward now also accepts the 'spared' stage 60

 7. Fill two more empty properties whose target records have slightly different names: Masters of Highreach 'LBMQ02' (Fulaz-Ahl's quest, used to tick his objective) and Tour of Duty's tracking spell (so M'zaad's tracking spell is removed afterwards)

 8. Door landing spots that were left sitting on top of the destination door: Garshakur Tailor exit (you appeared between the door and the wall) and the Putrid Cove Retreat entrance (you appeared facing the wall)

 9. Grave of the First: Fulaz-Ahl's barrier-breaking scene only removed the barrier when his 'cast Thunderbolt at the barrier marker' package finished; if it never finished he stood there 'busy' forever. The barrier is now removed when that part of the scene starts (patched scene script waits a few seconds and ends the scene if it is still hung)

10. Marks of a Fighter: Luzgrath asks for 'Stonebark Ale' and skulls, but only counted the vanilla Ale (rare in the valley) and the vanilla skull, not Lordbound's Stonebark ales or its own skull item; now any Stonebark drink or Ale, and either skull, count (and get taken)

11. The Lost Legion added at the end of Lordbound: the faction-epilogue quest picks a journal entry for stage 100, and the Imperial entry's only condition (The Lost Legion at stage 270) was flagged OR with nothing after it, which leaves that entry's test open-ended; the stray OR flag is removed

12. Chasing Dreams: the 'Find 3 Brainrot Mushrooms (x/3)' and 'Find 3 Nightshade (x/3)' objectives showed '[...]/3' because their counter globals were never added to the quest's Text Display Globals list

13. Models that point at files Lordbound never shipped (red '!' in game), repointed to an existing model: Ashlorn rib-cap pieces (4 placed), a blue crystal in Ysgramor's Folly (typo'd path), a Highrock Stop roof, Aedron's armor spaulders on women, and the Liquor of Rooting bottle

14. Enigmatic Origins: the Jagged Crown on the skeleton atop the throne in the Revenant's version of Highreach was a real, lootable crown placed as loose loot; it is now a static decoration with the same model

15. Removed the three spell tomes (Golden Sparks, Disintegrate the Undead, Fierce Flames) from the developers' 'Core Team Armoury' test room: those spells' effects use textures Lordbound never shipped (purple effects); the tome references are now initially disabled

16. The Trial Road / getting back into the valley: the Mor Khazgur Passage gate is a key-only lock that Mogus's scene unlocks once (stage 30), but the tunnel cell resets, which relocks the gate and leaves you shut out of (or into) the Druadach Valley; the gate now carries the base game's defaultUnlockOnLoad script (DD_OrcIntro, stage 30), so it unlocks itself every time it loads once that stage is done
